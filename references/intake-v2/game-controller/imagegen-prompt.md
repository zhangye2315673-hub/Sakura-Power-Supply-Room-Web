## Prompt

Create a clean 2 x 2 orthographic turn-sheet for an original SAKURA Crossing wireless game controller. Top-left strict front view, top-right strict right-side view, bottom-left strict rear view, bottom-right front-left three-quarter hero view. Show the exact same object at identical scale and consistent ground height in all four panels, isolated on a warm off-white background, with no dividers, text, labels or watermark.

Preserve the established controller identity and control layout: broad cream shoulder deck, deep central lower notch, two long outward-flared grips, large pink cross D-pad upper-left, four unlabeled pink face buttons in a diamond upper-right, symmetrical twin analog sticks low on the face, round home button and short horizontal select button at centre, two distinct shoulder layers per side, pink grip panels wrapping front-to-back with diagonal seams, rear battery cover, four rear screws and upper-centre rear port cover.

Upgrade it into a more exaggerated game prop without changing its overall envelope or functional anchor locations: stronger narrow waist, chunkier shoulder ridge, 15-20 percent more expressive grip flare and palm swell, larger faceted stick caps with deep thumb dishes, compact faceted button crowns, wedge-shaped bumper and trigger layers. Use deliberate LOW POLY construction: 6-12 sided profiles, broad planar facets, stepped bevel bands, crisp shell seams and readable undercuts; friendly but not soft or toy-like.

Use the SAKURA palette: warm cream shell, Sakura pink grips and controls, muted plum cavities and seams, cool lavender shadows and a tiny rose status lens. Use two-to-three-band toon lighting, minimal highlights and no realistic PBR texture. Apply a thick hand-inked outer contour with stable, subtly unequal width: heaviest on the outer silhouette, medium on structural seams and lighter on controls, approximately plus/minus 18 percent variation without sketchy temporal wobble.

The side view must show an ergonomic faceted S-curve and tapered handles rather than a rectangular slab. The rear view must preserve the independent battery cover, four screws, dual shoulder layers and centred port cover. No cable, hands, scene props, face, character features, logos, symbols, lettering, photorealism, glossy soft rubber, rounded chibi toy, mecha greebles, complex machinery, transparent shell, cropped parts or inconsistent control count.
