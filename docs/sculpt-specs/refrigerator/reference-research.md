# Refrigerator reference research

Research date: 2026-08-07. Target is an explicitly stylized SAKURA / low-poly approximation, not an exact reconstruction of a hidden interior from the supplied exterior sheet.

## Sources

- Real interior photograph: [LG refrigerator interior, Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LG_refrigerator_interior.jpg)
  - Observed a recessed back plane, side liners, full-depth shelves, stacked transparent drawers, food grouped by level, and multiple deep door bins.
  - The door storage remains mechanically attached to the door and rotates with it; bottles are supported by a base and a front retaining rail.
- Real product family: [Samsung all refrigerators](https://www.samsung.com/us/refrigerators/all-refrigerators/)
  - Used only for product-family proportion checks: deep cabinet, thick gasket/door slab, vertically repeated shelving and drawers.
- Stylized search set: [Sketchfab - stylized refrigerator](https://sketchfab.com/search?type=models&q=stylized%20refrigerator)
  - Search page was reachable. Common useful translation: large bevels, strong part silhouettes, small color palette, readable handles/hinges, and separated props rather than textured rectangles.
- Low-poly production search set: [Unity Asset Store - low poly fridge](https://assetstore.unity.com/?q=low%20poly%20fridge&orderBy=1)
  - Search page was reachable. Used to cross-check gameplay-scale readability and modular food-prop separation.
- Shape-language search set: [Pinterest - stylized refrigerator 3D](https://www.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=stylized%20refrigerator%203d)
  - Search page was reachable. Used for rounded, toy-like SAKURA proportions only; no geometry or textures were copied.

## Translation into this model

- Replace the closed solid cabinet with a frame made from rear, side, top, divider and bottom solids, leaving two real open cavities.
- Build each cavity from a back panel, two side liners, ceiling and floor; shelves extend into Z rather than acting as front plates.
- Add a volumetric produce drawer and four door bins with a base plus retaining rail.
- Food is built as named layered 3D assemblies: milk carton, fish, steak, storage box with contents, fruit cluster, carrot/broccoli vegetables and a drink bottle.
- Preserve the exterior reference's retro double-door proportions and right-side hinge inference.

## Uncertainty

The supplied exterior reference does not reveal the open interior. Shelf spacing, bin count and food selection therefore follow the cited real-interior evidence and are deliberately stylized. They must not be described as an exact match to the unseen original interior.
