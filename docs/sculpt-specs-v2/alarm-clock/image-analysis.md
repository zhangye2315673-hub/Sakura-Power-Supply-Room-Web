# Alarm clock v2 image analysis

## Suitability

Conditional pass. The isolated front, side, back, and three-quarter images provide
clear silhouette, depth, palette, and assembly evidence. They are archived v1
renders rather than GPT Image 2 references, so they guide a stylized upgrade and
do not support a claim of image-exact reconstruction.

## Observed hierarchy

- Macro: thick circular shell, two oversized bell domes, arched carry handle,
  and two outward-angled feet.
- Meso: stepped front rim, recessed ivory dial, bell lips/cavities/supports,
  top alarm lever, rear circular service plate, and two winding knobs.
- Micro: twelve ticks, separate hour/minute hands and center cap, two hammer
  assemblies, rear power inlet, and closed volumetric ringing feedback.

## Material and style reading

The shell uses warm Sakura pink painted hard-surface Toon values; the dial and
bells use warm cream; hands/cavities use cool violet. The v2 target deliberately
reduces radial segments to 10-12 sided forms, keeps two-to-three Toon bands, and
uses stable object-space ink at 0.0048 / 0.0041 / 0.0033 with 0.18 variation.
Transparent dial glass and hidden feedback effects do not receive the main hull ink.

## Frozen runtime evidence

Every existing pivot, socket, collider, destruction group, parent path, and local
rest transform remains authoritative. Bell shake, hammer motion, hand spin, body
shake, stereo waves, and vibration arcs must remain attached to the existing nodes.

## Remaining approximation

Hidden escapement, bell springs, shaft depths, and internal fasteners are omitted.
The faceted v2 shell and bell profiles are an intentional SAKURA game-style
interpretation within the v1 envelope.
