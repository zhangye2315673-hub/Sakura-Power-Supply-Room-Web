# Television v2 image analysis

## Intake

The admitted source is the archived v1 television front, side, back and three-quarter model-review captures. They are copied to `references/intake-v2/television/views/` as conditional fallback evidence. The environment had no Comfly or OpenAI credential variables, so no GPT Image 2 concept sheet was generated and no such provenance is claimed.

## Object-space observations

The television is a deep tabletop CRT assembly. The wide left display uses a layered dark cavity and strongly convex glass; a narrow Sakura control bay on the right carries one indexed selector, three thick channel keys, a separate power key and five speaker slots. The rear cap is visibly narrower than the front fascia and carries a recessed service panel, 3x14 upper vents, 3x5 lower vents, four fasteners and a grouped port bank. +Y is up, +Z is front and the floor remains y=0.

## v2 direction

The v2 build keeps the archived package envelope and every animated pivot/socket. The shell gains low-segment bevels, graphic side shoulders, inset accent blades, stronger screen brow/sill hierarchy and a twelve-tooth low-poly selector. Three stable ink tiers separate the cabinet, primary assemblies and detail. Programme geometry, transparent glass and screen effects remain free of thick outlines.

## Unknowns and limits

The internal CRT tube, electronics, speaker chamber, tuner mechanics and underside fastening remain inferred or omitted. The archived runtime contract is authoritative for hidden anchors, animation contact and collision dimensions.
