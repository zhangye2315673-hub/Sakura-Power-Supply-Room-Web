- Preserve every archived pivot/socket parent path and local transform within `1e-4`.
- Preserve width, height and depth within 2 percent and ground contact within `0.005`.
- Keep the platter/effect center at `[-0.35, 1.2, 0]` and retain exact tonearm cue/sweep behavior.
- Enlarge the record, tonearm hardware and speaker identity by 15-25 percent without leaving the old envelope.
- Use stable outline tiers `0.0048 / 0.0041 / 0.0033` with object-space variation `0.18`; transparent lid and music volumes are excluded.
- Keep triangles at or below `1.35x` v1 and browser draw calls at or below `1.20x` where comparable archived samples exist.
- Require four nonblank static views plus startup, climax and wind-down views with platter, stylus and volumetric music effects attached.
- Minimum overall visual score is `0.80`; identity silhouette, playback structure, animation contact and outline hierarchy must each score at least `0.82`.
