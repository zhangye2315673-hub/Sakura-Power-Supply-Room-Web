# SAKURA record player v2 image analysis

## Intake verdict

- Status: `conditional-fallback`.
- The four admitted 1200 px archive renders are valid object references but are not GPT Image 2 output.
- Front, side, back and three-quarter views reveal the complete exterior assembly; motor, belt, speaker cone and internal wiring remain hidden.

## Observed hierarchy

- Macro: low suitcase base, open framed lid, circular playback deck and front speaker fascia.
- Meso: faceted side cheeks, lid side spines, platter rim, tonearm base/arm/cartridge, control knob, grille cavity, rear hinges/handle/I/O.
- Micro: spindle, three record grooves, stylus, knob index, power lamp, eleven grille slats, speaker pulse badge and four feet.

## Material and style reading

- Cream and Sakura pink are matte three-band Toon plastics with cool-violet shadow tint.
- Vinyl is near-black with separated concentric geometry instead of a texture.
- Hardware is cool gray-purple metal; the inset lid panel is the only transparent shell material.
- The v2 target deliberately increases low-poly planes and outline hierarchy while preserving the archived proportions and animation skeleton.

## Identity and motion contract

- Identity-critical features are the oversized circular record, bent tonearm with visible cartridge/stylus, open suitcase lid and wide horizontal front grille.
- Frozen pivots are body, lid, platter, tonearm sweep, tonearm cue and control knob.
- The music effect center remains `[-0.35, 1.2, 0]`; waves and notes must rise from the platter, never from the old shell boundary.
- The visual model may exaggerate functional masses by 15-25 percent only inside the archived package.

## Inference limits

- Hidden motor and belt dimensions are not reconstructed.
- Exact lid wall thickness and hinge stop construction remain approximate.
- The archive lighting is used only for palette and form readability, not as proof of physical material values.
