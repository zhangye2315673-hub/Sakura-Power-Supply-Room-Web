# SAKURA Blender v2 — image analysis

## Suitability

- Verdict: pass. The IMAGEN sheet contains one isolated appliance in four coherent orthographic-like views; the deterministic admission gate accepted every crop as a distinct angle.
- Intended use: real-time browser appliance with one model-owned 5.2 second performance timeline.
- Authority split: the v2 sheet owns visible form language; `appliance-rig-v1-contract.json` owns bounds, pivots, sockets, colliders, node paths and reset transforms.
- Complexity: ultra-complex because transparent sorting, articulated lid/jar/dial/blades, whole and cut fruit, rising liquid, vortex highlight and two external mouth emitters must stay aligned.

## Macro → meso → micro hierarchy

- Macro: chunky Sakura-pink lower motor plinth; compressed cream trapezoid shoulder; faceted transparent jar; stepped lid; bold right-side angular handle.
- Meso: jar seat and collar; rolled rim; four-blade hub; oversized front speed dial; rear service plate; four feet; frozen fruit pile.
- Micro: eight molded jar ribs; lid knob; dial marker and status dot; five rear vents; two-pin inlet; seams; blade cant; stable unequal outline tiers.

## Object-space relationships

- The jar seat overlaps the upper motor shoulder and remains centered on `blender-jar-seat-pivot`.
- The jar is rooted at `blender-removable-jar-pivot`; its handle roots overlap the jar at the frozen upper and lower handle sockets.
- The lid seat remains centered above the jar. The lid overhang must leave the frozen left/right splash-mouth sockets visually unobstructed.
- The blade axis, fruit, liquid and cut chunks remain inside the jar coordinate frame; no visual geometry may be used to compensate by moving their animation pivots.
- The front dial is embedded in the lower shell at its archived pivot. Rear vents and the inlet remain on the negative-Z face.

## Material and outline response

- Warm cream and Sakura-pink molded shells use two-to-three-band Toon response with cool lavender shadow tint.
- The jar uses broad transparent planes, sparse bright ribs and a visible dark blade silhouette; it must not become an opaque white cup.
- Fruit remains opaque and chunky. Smoothie and splash volumes remain translucent and receive no main silhouette outline.
- Main opaque silhouettes use `0.0048`, structural seams `0.0041`, and detail contours `0.0033`, all with stable object-space variation `0.18`.

## Identity features

- Faceted upward-flaring jar and broad stepped lid.
- Right-side angular closed handle with clear negative space and two embedded roots.
- Two-band base with oversized mint front dial and small status point.
- Visible four-blade hub and compact strawberry/apple/orange/banana/blueberry pile.
- Rear service panel with five slots and centered low power inlet.

## Inference limits

- IMAGEN is not dimensional authority and is not allowed to move runtime anchors.
- Motor internals, interlock, blade coupling depth and cable routing remain hidden.
- The rendered transparent material is stylized, not an inverse-rendered physical glass claim.
- Powered smoothie and splash geometry are animation evidence, not static reference geometry.
