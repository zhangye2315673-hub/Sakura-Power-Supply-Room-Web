# Gumball Machine v2 image analysis

## Suitability

The generated four-view turn-sheet passes intake for a stylized procedural Three.js prop. It presents one unobstructed object, four useful views, a stable ground line, readable material families and inferable hidden structure. Exact manufacturing geometry is neither requested nor claimed.

## Classification and coordinate frame

- Primary type: coin-operated capsule toy vending machine.
- Domain: hard-surface object with transparent vessel and animated contents.
- Runtime: real-time browser game prop, interactive, animated, clickable and explodable.
- Object space: +Y up, +Z front, +X right; ground remains Y=0.

## Macro decomposition

1. A faceted near-spherical transparent globe dominates the upper silhouette.
2. A shallow twelve-sided pink lid dome, broad dark-pink rim and cream crown cap close the globe.
3. A double-lip pink globe seat bridges the transparent volume to the pedestal.
4. A cream tapered pedestal with broad pink lower plinth provides the footprint.
5. A front arched dispense frame, dark recessed cavity and projecting tray form the primary negative space.

## Meso decomposition

- Seventeen frozen two-piece capsules and their prize pivots occupy the globe around a central column and divider hub.
- A circular front crank stack has a seat, faceted face, cross grip and center boss.
- A right-side crank has an axle hub, bent low-segment arm and oversized faceted ball grip.
- The rear has a shallow service hatch, seam, cable notch and frozen power socket.
- Four feet remain separated at the existing contact points.

## Micro details

- Planar globe facets and restrained highlight planes, without thick black internal outlines.
- Lid radial facets, dark lower rim, cream crown and double-lip seat.
- Pedestal vertical facet bands and pink shoulder corner brackets.
- Capsule equator seams and opaque toy prizes visible through the clear upper halves.
- Faceted crank faces, indicator dot, tray lip and rear service inset.
- Stable object-space outline variation with three thickness tiers.

## Material observations

- Cream polymer: matte toon response, warm albedo, cool lavender shadow tint.
- Sakura pink polymer: slightly lower roughness than cream, with pale pink upper planes and deep plum-pink rims.
- Globe and capsule clear shells: transparent tinted polymer; depth write disabled; no main outline on overlapping interiors.
- Capsule lower shells: cyan, pink, lemon and mint pastel families.
- Cavity and rubber: deep desaturated plum for negative-space legibility.
- Prize colors: saturated but controlled coral, lavender, ochre and mint.

## Identity-defining features

- Globe-to-pedestal proportion and lid/seat rings.
- Arched front dispensing opening and tray.
- Front crank plus side crank, both attached at the frozen pivots.
- Multiple colored two-piece capsules with solid prizes.
- Rear hatch and cable socket.

## Runtime evidence overrides

The generated turn-sheet changes internal capsules into bowl-like display cups. That visual invention is rejected for the runtime model. The archived v1 model and rig contract remain authoritative for all 17 capsule pivots, prize pivots, output capsule halves, success bursts, sockets, transforms, animation trajectory and reset state. New visual geometry must be authored under those frozen nodes rather than moving the animation controller.

## Hidden-side confidence

Rear hatch shape is high confidence from the back view. Internal gear train, divider wheel, anti-double-feed flap, underside fasteners and wiring remain unobserved; only the existing restrained divider hub and cable socket are retained. No additional mechanism is claimed.
